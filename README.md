# A-chair-made-of-a-5mm-steel-sheet-cut-out-with-laser-and-bended.
The chair is made of a 5mm steel sheet cut out with laser and bended in just 3 sections with 5mm bending radius.  
If you decide to use a different material than steel it is recommended to double check the durability of the chair modify the thickness and therefore  modify the bending radius too. Although you better check the weight capacity on this one too. You will need a 410mm * 1360mm sheet of steel with the thickness of 5mm, laser cutter and a bending machine with with 5mm radius of bending.
 The design is open source can be modified and changed as one may wish. I have not checked the durability and weight capacity.
